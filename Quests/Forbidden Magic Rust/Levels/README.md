# Level 1 — The Awakening of the Rust Spellbook

> [!abstract] Quest Summary
> You are not here to conquer Rust yet.
> You are here to **enter the archive**, **open the spellbook**, and **begin the first true study** of its runes.

## Lore

> [!quote] The Tale of the New Spellbook
> In the elder age of machines, wizard-engineers shaped their wonders with the ancient magic of **C**. It was fierce, glorious, and perilous: swift enough to move the gears of empires, powerful enough to command iron minds, and dangerous enough to consume the careless in flame.
>
> For the old magic demanded sacrifice. A misplaced rune, a borrowed pointer not returned, a buffer left unguarded—such failures could unleash hidden curses upon entire systems. Castles of logic would fall not from flawed ambition, but from the quiet betrayal of memory itself.
>
> Then came the modern era: sky-ships across the heavens, engines with the hearts of stars, and machines woven ever deeper into civilization. In such an age, power alone was no longer enough. The world demanded **power with trust**.
>
> So from the forge emerged a new spellbook: **Rust**.
>
> Rust was not born to mock the old ways, but to complete them. It kept the strength of C—closeness to the metal, mastery over performance, and dominion over low-level systems—but bound that strength within stricter laws.
>
> Its runes spoke of **ownership**. Its wards declared **borrowing**. Its guardians enforced **lifetimes**. And at its gates stood the **Compiler**, stern and tireless, refusing passage to spells that would shatter the world.
>
> Many young wizards cursed these laws at first. Yet those who endured discovered the deeper truth: **Rust does not weaken the mage. It transforms the mage.**

## Index of the Rust Questline

> [!info] Journey Through the Archive
> A roadmap through the official Rust documentation, retold as a wizard’s ascent through the spellbook.

- [ ] **Level 1 — The Awakening of the Rust Spellbook**
	- Focus: why Rust exists, what makes it different, and the first core runes
	- Rust Book path: Chapters 1–6

- [ ] **Level 2 — The Trials of Ownership**
	- Focus: ownership, borrowing, references, slices, memory safety
	- Rust Book path: Chapters 4–5

- [ ] **Level 3 — Forging Forms and Fates**
	- Focus: structs, enums, pattern matching, expressive data design
	- Rust Book path: Chapters 5–6

- [ ] **Level 4 — The Order of Modules**
	- Focus: crates, modules, paths, `use`, project organization
	- Rust Book path: Chapters 7 and 14

- [ ] **Level 5 — The Keeper of Collections**
	- Focus: strings, vectors, hash maps, standard library tools
	- Rust Book path: Chapter 8

- [ ] **Level 6 — Echoes, Errors, and Prophecy**
	- Focus: `Result`, `Option`, recoverable failure, panics, resilient spells
	- Rust Book path: Chapter 9

- [ ] **Level 7 — The Way of Generics**
	- Focus: generics, traits, lifetimes
	- Rust Book path: Chapter 10

- [ ] **Level 8 — The Invisible Contracts**
	- Focus: testing, documentation, clarity, trustworthiness
	- Rust Book path: Chapters 11 and 14

- [ ] **Level 9 — Cargo and the Forge of Projects**
	- Focus: packages, dependencies, workspaces, profiles, publishing
	- Rust Book path: Chapters 12 and 14
	- Companion text: [Cargo Book](https://doc.rust-lang.org/cargo/)

- [ ] **Level 10 — The Many-Shaped Spell**
	- Focus: iterators, closures, smart pointers, advanced patterns
	- Rust Book path: Chapters 13, 15, 17, and 18

- [ ] **Level 11 — The Concurrency Wars**
	- Focus: fearless concurrency, threads, message passing, shared state
	- Rust Book path: Chapter 16

- [ ] **Level 12 — The Forbidden Chambers**
	- Focus: unsafe Rust, advanced traits, macros, low-level power
	- Rust Book path: Chapters 19 and 20

- [ ] **Final Trial — The Engine Beyond the Horizon**
	- Focus: build a real project from your gathered knowledge
	- Suggested outcome: CLI tool, parser, tiny game system, or systems utility

### Suggested Route

> [!tip] Recommended Study Order
> - Read **The Rust Programming Language** as your main spellbook.
> - Use **Rust By Example** when a concept feels too abstract.
> - Use **Rustlings** as training chambers between levels.
> - Use the **Cargo Book** when your studies become craft.

## Quest Objective

> [!question] What must the Wizard do?
> In this first level, your task is not conquest, but **exploration**.
> You shall journey through the documentation of Rust and learn the foundations of the spellbook.

### Path Through the Archive

- [ ] **Discover the origin of Rust**
	- Learn why Rust was created
	- Understand what problems of old C-style magic it seeks to solve

- [ ] **Study the Core Runes**
	- Variables
	- Functions
	- Control flow
	- Ownership
	- Borrowing
	- Structs
	- Enums
	- Pattern matching

- [ ] **Meet the Compiler Guardian**
	- Do not fear its warnings
	- Treat each error as prophecy showing how the spell must improve

- [ ] **Compare the Old Magic to the New**
	- Reflect on how Rust differs from C and similar low-level languages
	- Ask: what does Rust protect, and what freedom does it demand in return?

- [ ] **Record your findings**
	- Keep notes as though writing your own apprentice grimoire
	- Write down new words, strange rules, and surprising spells
