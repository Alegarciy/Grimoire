# Gitin

## About
Gitin is an organized way to collaborate on open source. We believe in de-centralization of cloud services, collaboration without frontiers. Are you a Nerd or Geek? Seeking a community? Gitin!

## What is Gitin?
+ Cross cloud git repositories search engine.
+ Collaboration organizer
+ Open Source Scheduler
+ Community public hearing place 
+ A repository search engine from hell

## What is it not?
+ A social media
+ A branded corporate garbage 
+ A sponsored project
+ Tool Agnostic

## What do we believe?

### Organization
There is only one way to organize and is our way.

1. Only stack identical items, unique items must never be stacked. Stacking unique items invite chaos
2. Kit your sh!t. Descentralize your mess with multiple kits. Do not migrate your kits. The evil twin of kits are junk drawer
3. Name and create your organization talisman. We reveared organization talismans.
4. Learn to build containers. Even chaotic container look organized, don't be intimidated. Containers is the Genesis of your organized life.
5. Customize, encourage organization by customization. Customization allows us to clear our space and stay organized.

### Details Matters
Each detail is a burden that serves a specific purpose.

1. Date everything, for forensic biographical purposes.
2. Do list to perfection, having a philosophy is considered the wrong philosophy.
